Golapada for Schools

Golapada is a set of free space-science labs that run in any web browser. Every number in them is worked out from real physics and checked against real missions, many of them Indian. This page tells you which lab fits which NCERT chapter, how to run one in a single 40-minute period, and gives you eight lesson plans you can use tomorrow.

Which lab for which chapter

Chapters are from the NCERT textbooks (2026–27 reprints) used in CBSE schools. Pick your class. Class 9 now uses the new book Exploration, which has no chapter on gravitation, so the orbit and gravity labs are listed under Class 11.

One lab, one 40-minute period

Minutes 0–5Warm-up

Ask the question in the plan. Let students guess before anyone opens a screen.

Minutes 5–20Explore

Pairs work through the lab with the exact settings in the plan. Or drive it yourself on the projector.

Minutes 20–30Discuss

Use the discussion questions. Ask pairs to explain what they saw, not just the answer.

Minutes 30–37Exit ticket

Two or three short questions on paper. The answers are in each plan.

Minutes 37–40Take home

Set the matching worksheet, or an observation for tonight.

Only one screen? Every lab works on a projector: you move the sliders and the class calls out what to try. Every lab also has a “How far to trust it” table. Older students can use it to talk about how models are checked against real data.

Ready lesson plans

Tap a plan to open it. Printing this page prints every plan with its answers.

No reliable internet? Take it offline

Download every lab as one zip file and copy it to the school computers or a pen drive. Unzip it and open index.html in any browser. No installing needed.

Download golapada-offline.zip

Offline, the labs still calculate everything. Three things need the internet: the website’s fonts (a plain font is used instead), the live Earth photographs in Earth from Above, and the tracking library Satellite Watch and Tonight’s Sky use. The satellite positions inside the zip are from the day you download it and drift out of date within a week or two, so download a fresh copy now and then.

Golapada is an independent educational project. It is not made by, affiliated with or endorsed by ISRO, NCERT, CBSE or NASA. Mission figures come from public sources named on each lab’s page. Chapter titles follow the NCERT textbooks; check the edition your school uses.
