Worksheets

Short worksheets that turn real space missions into school physics problems. Each question names the lab where students can check their answer by changing the numbers themselves. Print one per student, or open the answer key for yourself.

Topic

Values used: G·M(Earth) = 3.986 × 10¹⁴ m³/s², Earth’s radius 6,371 km (6,378 km at the equator), g = 9.81 m/s², speed of light 3.00 × 10⁸ m/s. Mission figures come from the sources cited in each lab.
