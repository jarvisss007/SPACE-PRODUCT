Golapada

Space science you can play with. 50 interactive labs built on real mission data, from tonight’s sky to the edge of the galaxy.

Golapada, “the chapter on the sphere”, is the part of Aryabhata’s Aryabhatiya (499 CE) where he explained the turning Earth, the sky and eclipses.

Why you can trust it

Real numbersMission figures come from ISRO, NASA, ESA and JPL, cited at the bottom of every lab.
Checked against realityEach lab ends with a table comparing the model with what really happened, including where it falls short.
Live where it mattersSatellite orbits refresh every day from public tracking data; the planets and Moon are worked out for the moment you open the page.
Free and openNo account, no ads, no tracking. It works on any phone with a browser.
